About

Security at the boundaries between systems.

I am Yongyang Lv, an Assistant Professor at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Tianjin University. My research interests include network security, data security, blockchain security, privacy-enhancing technologies, and secure data sharing.

My recent work explores cross-chain authentication and interoperability, privacy-preserving data exchange, and secure healthcare data management.

Research

Research interests

Building dependable mechanisms for data, identity, and trust across system boundaries.

01

Network & Data Security

Secure data management, access control, identity authentication, and privacy protection in distributed systems.

02

Blockchain & Cross-chain Security

Cross-chain authentication, blockchain interoperability, protocol design, and formal security analysis.

03

Privacy-Enhancing Technologies

Proxy re-encryption, privacy-enhancing encryption, and cryptographic mechanisms for secure data sharing.

04

Healthcare & IoT Security

Secure personal health record sharing, blockchain-enabled healthcare, and trustworthy IoT data exchange.
